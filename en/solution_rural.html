PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

The Zhangmutou rural domestic wastewater project uses integrated treatment equipment with a capacity of 20 m³/d and commenced operation in 2026.

The integrated treatment process combines screening, equalization, A/O biological contact oxidation, secondary clarification and disinfection.
