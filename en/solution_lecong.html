PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

The Shunde Lecong Wastewater Treatment Plant in the Longjiang–Lecong area has a treatment capacity of 60,000 m³/d and commenced operation in May 2025.

Modified UCT biological treatment is followed by continuous sand filtration and contact disinfection before discharge to Jiushachong.

TREATMENT PROCESS

Wastewater Treatment Process

  1. 01

    Coarse Screens & Inlet Pumping

  2. 02

    Fine Screens & Vortex Grit Removal

  3. 03

    Modified UCT Bioreactor

    Anaerobic–anoxic–aerobic treatment for enhanced nitrogen and phosphorus removal

  4. 04

    Secondary Clarification

  5. 05

    Fine Screening & Lift Pumping

  6. 06

    Continuous Sand Filter

    Advanced filtration for suspended solids (SS) and total phosphorus (TP) removal

  7. 07

    Contact Disinfection Tank

  8. 08

    Discharge to Jiushachong
