Products / Design & Configuration

Product Features & Benefits

Guwei rainwater harvesting systems use factory-produced, high-density injection-moulded PP modules to create modular storage tanks as an alternative to conventional concrete tanks.

Key Benefits

  • Load-bearing, ageing-resistant modules with acid and alkali resistance for long-term underground use.
  • Flexible assembly for different storage volumes, with less excavation and shorter construction periods.
  • Complete filtration, first-flush diversion, disinfection and control systems support rainwater reuse for landscape irrigation and road washing.

Applications

Suitable for sponge-city infrastructure, municipal stormwater storage, property developments, industrial parks and car parks.

Project Partnerships

Manufactured in-house, with design proposals, construction drawings and technical handover. We welcome contractors and main contractors.
