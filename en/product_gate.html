Products / Structure & Applications

Product Features & Benefits

Copper-seated Cast Iron Round Gate

A one-piece ductile iron body with copper seating surfaces supports reliable sealing, smooth operation and a compact installation footprint.

Applications
Wastewater treatment plants, lifting stations, underground pipe networks and inspection chambers.
Main Uses
Flow isolation, water level regulation and draining for maintenance.
Lifting Equipment
Manual or electric screw hoists can be supplied.
Media & Corrosion Protection
Select for the wastewater and medium. For acidic or alkaline media, suitability and corrosion protection requirements must be confirmed.

Steel Plane Sliding Gate

A welded carbon steel structure with heavy-duty corrosion protection and support rollers reduces operating friction. Its large flow area and structural strength suit high-flow applications.

Applications
Rivers, irrigation canals, reservoirs and water diversion works.
Main Uses
Water level regulation, flood discharge and flow isolation.
Lifting Equipment
Supplied with winch hoists; large non-standard sizes can be customized.
Structural Review
Support type, guide rail configuration and installation dimensions are confirmed against the project drawings.
