Compact Busway

Guwei compact busway combines an aluminium alloy enclosure with copper conductors in a compact structure for high-current power distribution, offering an alternative to conventional cable tray wiring.

Product Features & Benefits

Key Benefits

  • High current capacity:A range of current ratings, controlled temperature rise and stable conductivity for high-power distribution applications.
  • Effective heat dissipation:The aluminium alloy enclosure provides a large heat dissipation area to reduce operating temperatures and support service life.
  • Flexible installation:Modular sections, including straight lengths, elbows, tees and reducers, enable efficient on-site assembly.
  • Multiple options:Standard compact, fire-resistant and waterproof busway options suit indoor installations, risers, basements and other environments.
  • Project support:Type test reports and qualification documents are available, with support for detailed drawings, site measurement and technical guidance.

Applications

Industrial plants, commercial complexes, hospitals, schools, data centres, housing developments and municipal MEP EPC projects.

Project Partnerships

Direct from the manufacturer, with fabrication to your drawings. We welcome cooperation with MEP contractors and power distribution service providers.
