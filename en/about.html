Company Profile

Water treatment R&D and manufacturing

2015Established
Zengcheng, Guangzhou, China

Established in 2015 at 76 Fuzhong Road, Zhongxin Town, Zengcheng District, Guangzhou, Guwei develops and manufactures water treatment equipment, smart water management platforms and seawater desalination systems for municipal, industrial, livestock, healthcare and domestic applications.

Guwei also plans industrial systems and manufactures complete electrical cabinets, providing electrical design, electromechanical installation, commissioning, operation and after-sales services across environmental protection, electronics, chemicals, manufacturing, new energy and automation.
