4. Prepare a comparable cost basis
Use the same reporting period, influent conditions and actual volume treated.
Electricity: consumption records or operating assumptions; tariff; actual load and operating hours.
Chemicals and consumables: test or operating consumption; purchase prices; replacement intervals.
Sludge and other residuals: quantities, moisture content, transport and disposal quotations, and scope included.
Labor, testing and maintenance: staffing, frequency, maintenance plan and included costs.
State whether equipment purchase, civil works and depreciation are excluded or accounted for separately.
Calculate unit treatment cost using agreed costs and actual volume treated within the same period. Figures expressed per tonne are not comparable unless their scope and basis match.

5. Technical and delivery information to request
Design basis, process description, equipment list, operating conditions, operating cost assumptions, supply boundaries, installation and commissioning responsibilities, exclusions and follow-up verification documents.

6. Check reference projects
Are project type and scale comparable?
Are the contract scope, influent conditions and treatment objectives clear?
Are relevant acceptance, test, operating and cost records available?
