PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

This project supplies wastewater treatment station equipment for the launch area external utilities and off-site MEP installation works of Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site (Phase II).

Treatment combines chemical conditioning, coagulation and flocculation, flotation and filtration, with online effluent oil monitoring and automatic PLC control.
