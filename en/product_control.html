Products / Design & Configuration

Product Features & Benefits

Key Benefits

  • Integrated monitoring and remote access:Monitoring, alarms and equipment control in one system, with real-time status and fault reporting accessible from a phone or computer.
  • Automatic control and variable-speed operation:Automatic start/stop and variable-frequency adjustment based on level, pressure and flow reduce operator attendance, energy use and operating costs.
  • Open interfaces and retrofit compatibility:RS-485 and Ethernet interfaces support supervisory systems and third-party platforms, including upgrades to existing installations.

Applications

Automation for municipal water and drainage, prefabricated pumping stations, wastewater stations, residential booster supply, industrial circulating water and rainwater reuse.

Project Partnerships

Manufactured in-house, with control schemes, electrical schematics and installation drawings tailored to the process. We provide programming, commissioning and technical handover for contractors and main contractors.
